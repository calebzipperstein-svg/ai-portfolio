How I Think
Most people use AI to go faster.
I use it to stay human.
01

I start by identifying the real bottleneck, not the obvious one. At Papaya, the problem was not effort. It was context switching and specification fidelity across 5 product lines, 28 open deals, and no SDR support.

02

Before prompting anything, I designed a pipeline. What data goes in, what judgment is required, what the output looks like, and how success is measured. Then I built it.

03

The result: each morning my system pulls from 5 live data sources, ranks my 15 highest-signal targets, routes outreach by channel, and generates call preps for every meeting before I open my laptop.

04

That freed me to do what AI cannot do. Be present with clients, ask better questions, open markets, and close deals. The architecture is portable to any context where human judgment is the scarce resource.

05

The system I built was not a sales tool. It was an operating layer. The internal-comms skill, the GTM frameworks for Africa and India, the weekly audit that maintains the whole stack. I was building product while doing my day job, and the people around me adopted it. That is the part I find most interesting.
