Philosophy
"The right AI architecture doesn't just make you faster.
It makes the hardest parts of the job feel manageable, so you can spend your time doing what actually moves the needle: being present with clients, asking better questions, and closing."
01

Most people treat AI as a tool to operate. They start executing immediately. I start by identifying the real bottleneck.

02

For me, the bottleneck was not writing better emails. It was specification fidelity and context loss between data sources. So before prompting anything, I designed a pipeline: what data goes in, what judgment is required, what the output looks like, and how success is measured.

03

The result: each morning my Daily Briefing pulls from 5 live sources, ranks my 15 highest-signal targets, routes outreach by channel, and generates call preps for every external meeting before I open my laptop. The inbox-organizer routes emails using live Salesforce opportunity matching. The weekly-organization task audits the whole system every Sunday.

04

I am not selling prompts. I am selling the judgment about what to build, why, and how to measure whether it is working. That architecture is portable to any sales motion, ops function, or business context.
